## Concrete settlement

Source ownership exists before unsafe-file constructor rollback. All explicit paths
consume the descriptor before close, check the real syscall result and never retry.
Stage-create refusal closes the already-owned source. Ordinary phase refusal/cancel
closes the internal observation pin after trusted phase return and before eligible
owned-stage cleanup. If source close is uncertain, cleanup is skipped and the original
stage/needed outer grants remain under typed review. An unresolved phase remains the
operation cause when close also refuses; its own workers/readers are not declared
settled by closing this independent transaction observation pin.

After actual exclusive rename, SourceSettlementFailure includes actual Published
directory/verified bytes/member count. The concrete operation retains that committed
locator and grants even when errors are dropped. It neither discards the committed
directory nor classifies it as no publication. Late cancellation still reports actual
success when all qualified ownership settles. Additional bounded energy expiry is
not settlement, access release or cleanup authority. Consumed uncertain descriptor
numbers are never retried; failed OS close does not prove release.

DEBUG reports follow actual successful closes. They model reported uncertainty,
not spontaneous OS faults. Fake grant ordering is not actual sandbox grant/revocation.
Controlled generated registry isolation after joined helpers and known actual closes
is not a production recovery-release or genuine group-settlement API.

## Remaining staging scope

D099 parent/stage descriptors, verification component descriptors and directory-stream
close outcomes are not changed or qualified here. Creation rollback, verification
abort/EOF, exclusive rename, ordinary cleanup and published-state propagation need
one concrete stage-owner settlement unit. Such a unit must accumulate close uncertainty
without skipping worker joins or deleting unsettled stages, and must preserve actual
committed results after rename. It cannot infer safe cleanup from a callback, energy
expiry or a joined direct child alone. No all-descriptor readiness claim follows D135.

Full D109-D114 source-dependent original semantics remain required. Version-zero
archive is not portable/stable import, immutable snapshot or persisted producer
provenance. Fixed-decoder sample agreement is not independently remeasured pixels,
rendering/PQ/EL/every-field interpretation or crop/resize/edit conversion. DeveloperID,
hardened loading/distribution, sandbox grants/revocation, recovery, volume/blocked-I/O/
power and edited-picture gates remain open. No whole-program completion.
