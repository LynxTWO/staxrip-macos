# Exact packet timing for the Dolby HDR10 copy consumer

D177 implements the timing portion of the planned M2 source-to-result encoded verifier. It does not enable conversion or publication. Existing native Matroska packet observations contain signed PTS and optional BlockDuration, but no DTS; the SDR packet comparator does not provide this exact-copy contract. Its behavior is unchanged.

The dedicated streaming FFprobe reader requires explicit signed PTS, signed DTS, positive duration, bounded size and SHA-256 for every record. Missing/N/A, duplicate fields, malformed hashes, overflow, incomplete final lines and count/line limits refuse. Encoded order and duplicate/nonmonotonic PTS are retained. Separate domain-prefixed, fixed-width sequence digests support native whole-packet binding and exact source/output timing comparison. Comparison requires the same admitted time base; it neither synthesizes DTS nor applies a rounding tolerance. The passed time base still needs source admission and identity binding by the eventual consumer.

The same existing ToolRunner joins before parser completion and opts into checked reader closes. A shared ownership error wins over parsing/cancellation; reported reader uncertainty also carries the parser cause. The caller must retain needed source/candidate access and staging on that marker. No new worker, manifest file, ownership registry or cleanup authority is added. Source/tool admission, grants, staging, remaining descriptor roles and universal descendant settlement are not qualified here.

Four tests cover signed extrema, duplicate order, DTS/duration changes, independent packet-digest construction, invalid framing/bounds and generated helper/close refusals. The final generated eight-picture source is asserted as 3840×2160 Main10 PQ/BT.2020 at 24000/1001, then copied and compared; both actual selected-stream time bases are read before comparison. This fixture has no Dolby metadata: it qualifies timing transport, not P7/MEL/CM2.9 admission or Dolby removal. Generated review roots are retained. Reports follow successful Foundation close calls; they are not spontaneous OS close faults. The initial three-test selection passed in 0.938 seconds; expanded four tests passed in 0.869 seconds. Three existing test warning sites appeared during the initial rebuild; no new warning site was introduced.

Required next gates remain the complete native kept-NAL/configuration projection, source/output packet binding, no-Dolby output declarations, complete decoded frames/static metadata/chroma and picture association, concrete Batch ownership and exclusive publication, then the isolated native journey and usable result.
